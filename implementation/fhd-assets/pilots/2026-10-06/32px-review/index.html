All other assets —32px farmer density

320 transparent PNGs: buildings, defenses, trees, animals, objects, furniture, crops, terrain, effects and interface. Includes upgrades, damaged/destroyed states, connected wall/fence pieces and building slices. Farmer and enemies are excluded; the separate contact shadow is included.

32px refers to the selected farmer's artwork detail, not a32×32 box for every object. These existing assets already use the matching density: one native pixel becomes4 screen pixels at FHD1920×1080. A16px tile becomes64px on screen. Furniture and trees retain their larger proportions. This page displays the original PNGs unchanged.

All frames and states are shown individually. Trees/animals and fireplace motion use shaders in the game; this gallery shows their source art. Terrain atlas and building slices are supporting assets. This is an unlit asset gallery, not a screenshot of the game.
