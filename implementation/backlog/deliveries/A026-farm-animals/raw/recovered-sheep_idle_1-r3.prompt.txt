## What it is for

Task 0185 fills the barn. Sheep give wool, pigs dig truffles, and every animal wanders, eats and sleeps near its building as a view-only state. The player sees them by day on the grass round the barn, a few cells from the 32px farmer. They also sleep beside the barn in the dark evening before the farmer goes to bed; at night `DAY_ONLY` hides them. At 1080p a sheep must read as a cream wool body with a dark face, and a pig as a round peach-pink body with a snout. Both must be clearly smaller than the farmer and bigger than a chicken, by day and after the night relight. This pack sets the look of the animal family: A027 (cow and chicken) and A028 (dog) match it.

   - Pig: "a farm pig with a round barrel body of peach-pink skin, a flat snout disc, small folded ears, a short curled tail, short legs, dark hooves; no mud, spots, collar or clothes."
2. **Tool.** Built-in ImageGen with `transparent_background=true` on every call, one subject per call. Attach:
   - the farmer side-view frame (role: scale and outline reference);
   - `docs/reference-sheet.png` (role: style reference);
   - `sprites/props/cow.png` (role: palette and camera reference, not an edit target);
   - after the master is accepted, the master (role: identity master, or edit target for edits).
3. **Order per species.**
   1. Generate the standing master and reduce it.
   2. Check the reduced master beside the farmer before you make anything else.
   3. Make the stationary frames as ImageGen edits, each one changing only the pose: `idle_1` and `eat_0` from the master, `eat_1` from `eat_0`, `sleep_0` from the master, and `sleep_1` from `sleep_0`.
   4. Make `walk` (and the pig's `dig`) as one STRIP call each, with the master attached.
   
   Run at most 3 CORRECTION revisions per asset.
4. **Reduce** with the mode A recipe (`render_source` in `tools/import_art.py`, imported read-only).
   - **Master.**
     - `crop_xywh` is the measured visible box.
     - Pick `content_scale` so the standing body lands inside the height target.
     - The default anchor puts the hooves on row 29.
   - **Edits.** Reuse the master's `crop_xywh` and `content_scale` when the edit kept the framing: the hoof baseline and the rump line stay within 1% of the crop. Otherwise revise the edit.
   - **Strips.**
     - Cut the cells with measured boxes.
     - Every frame uses a box of the same size at the same offset in its cell, with its bottom edge on the strip's measured hoof baseline.
     - Use one `content_scale` per strip, picked so the reduced torso matches the master (Â±1 px).
     - A per-frame vertical crop offset is allowed only to land the contact row on 29, and it is recorded in the recipe.
   - If reduction erases the sheep's face or the pig's snout, regenerate the source. Never patch native pixels.
