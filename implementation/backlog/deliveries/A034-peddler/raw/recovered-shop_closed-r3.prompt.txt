## What it is for

The stall gets a peddler who keeps hours, 08:00 to 17:00 (game task 0190): he
walks in from the farm's edge in the morning, stands at the stall, waves and
talks while the player trades, and walks off in the evening; outside his hours
the stall is shut. He is the only other person the player meets, so at 1080p he
stands beside the blue 32px farmer at **exactly the farmer's density and
height**, with an identity of his own: never the farmer's straw hat and blue
clothes, never a zombie's rot greens. The closed stall must read as "shut for
the night" at a glance, by dusk and by night, and match today's stall exactly.
