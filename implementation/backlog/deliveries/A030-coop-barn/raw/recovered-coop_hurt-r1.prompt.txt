# A030 - The coop and the barn

- **Status:** generating
- **For game task:** 0212
- **Dressed by game tasks:** 0183, 0186

- **Mode:** A - raw ImageGen sources, Claude reduces them to the 42-colour palette
- **Size of the job:** 2 buildings x 3 states (whole, hurt, wreck) = 6 frames; 2 masters + 4 state edits

## What it is for

Animals get homes (game task 0183): chickens live in the coop, cows, sheep and
pigs in the barn. The player builds them by day near the workshop, collects
eggs and milk at their doors, and at night the animals sleep inside while
breakers go for the buildings (0186). A wrecked barn stays standing, produces
nothing and keeps its animals until it is repaired by day, so the wreck must
read as "broken, out of use" without collapsing. At 1080p they are warm timber
farm buildings at **full density** (1 art px = 1 logical px), clearly smaller
than the house, which stays the landmark; by night their lantern and windows
are lit by the runtime.

## How to make it

1. Prompt = PROMPTS.md BASE (braces: canvas and pivot from the table) + the
   PRODUCTION_BUILDING section with `{coop | barn}` and the function cues from
   the table in place of its station list, + CORRECTION for revisions. Add:
   "Full-density pixel art: about {56 | 88} art pixels across the whole
   building; footprint {3x2 | 5x4} cells of 16 px. Front door centred. Window
   glass unlit and separate. No animals, fence, ground, shadow or text."
2. Masters `coop` and `barn`, `transparent_background=true`, 2 candidates each,
   with `assets/art_source/buildings/house/whole.png` attached as "material,
   camera and lighting style reference". Pick by the native reduction.
3. Hurt and wreck are ImageGen **edits of the accepted whole raw** (edit
   target), one state per call, "preserve framing, dimensions, ground baseline,
   door and window positions; change only the damage", the way task 0011 made
   `house/hurt.png`.
4. Export with the mode A recipe: per building one crop box (the union of its
   three states' opaque bboxes), one `content_scale`, one `content_anchor_xy`,
   so unchanged walls land on the same pixels in all three states. Lowest
   opaque row `pivot_y - 1`, centred on `pivot_x`. Measure the points on the
   exported native frames.
