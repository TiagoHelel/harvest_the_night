# HARVEST THE NIGHT - read me first

Thank you for playing this. It is the whole year of the game, a few weeks
before it is finished: every season, every night and every boss are in it,
and some of the art is not. Below is what would be most useful from you.

## What it is

You farm by day: crops that grow a day at a time when you water them, a pond
to fish, a cliff to mine, trees to chop, animals in a coop and a barn,
machines that turn what you grow into something worth more, a stall with a
peddler, and a shipping bin that pays you the next morning.

When you go to bed - the bed in the farmhouse, or the GO TO BED button - the
farmer sleeps and the farm becomes a tower defence. You are the guardian: a
cursor over the farm. Build with the bar at the bottom, press **START** when
you are ready, and keep the house standing until dawn at 06:00. Stay up too
late and he passes out at 02:00, and the build step is short.

The year is four seasons of fourteen days. Each season opens a new side of
the farm, new enemies and new defences, and ends with a boss on its last
night. A night you lose costs you - the horde takes some of what you stored
and tramples what is in its way - but the year carries on. Beat the winter
boss and the year is won; the game then offers you Year 2.

## The most useful stretch

**Spring 1 to the Giant, on Spring 14.** That is the first season: the first
night, every spring enemy, the first Full Moon and the first boss. It takes
two to three hours. Play it once without being told anything: start it, work
out what to do, and stop when you get stuck or bored. Do not ask me what a
button does - if you cannot tell, that is the finding, and the moment you
ask, it stops being one.

If you get properly stuck, press **F1** and then carry on however you like.

## F1 leaves a note

Any time something is confusing, annoying, surprising or broken, press **F1**.
It writes a numbered marker into a log file with the time on it. It does not
ask you to type anything - it is one key, so you can press it mid-fight.

Afterwards, tell me what note 1, note 2 and note 3 were about. That is the
whole feedback format. Three sentences is plenty.

## What to try

- **Go to bed early one night and late the next** - and once, stay up until
  he passes out, to see what it costs.
- **Play a night with the mouse and one with a gamepad**, if you have one:
  the cursor, the bar, START and the powers.
- **Take your time before START.** The night does not begin until you press
  it.
- **Put something in the shipping bin** and see what it pays in the morning.
- **Fish.** The rod is in your tools from the first morning.

## What to ignore

- **The farmer's missing animations.** Going to bed, sleeping, waking and
  passing out, his reactions, tilling, planting, watering, harvesting,
  chopping, mining, collecting, carrying, crafting, trading and repairing
  are still stand-ins: he slides or stands still while the work happens.
  The rest of the art is the game's.
- **Sound balance.** The mix is not done.
- **The tech tree's prices.** Every node does what it says; whether each is
  worth what it costs is still being tuned.
- **The version in the corner.** It still says prototype.
- **The orange box at the start of each run.** That is this build, not
  the game. It goes away after a few seconds.

## What to report, in order of how much it helps

1. **Where you stopped playing and why.** The most valuable sentence there is.
2. **Anything you expected to be able to do and could not.**
3. **Anything you did by accident.**
4. **Three questions only you can answer:**
   - Did the lines that teach the first week make sense the first time you
     read them?
   - Was a night long enough - or too long - for what you wanted to do in it?
   - After the morning's field, were the pond, the cliff and the animals
     worth doing for their own sake, or only for what they buy for the
     night?
5. Crashes. The game writes a folder for those and tells you where it is.
6. Typos, spacing, a label in the wrong place - in either language.

## The log

Everything above is written to a file on your own machine:

    %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\HARVEST THE NIGHT\playtest\

It is plain text: the days and nights you played, when you went to bed, how
long you built before START, how each night ended, and how long each took -
no name, no addresses, nothing from outside the game's own folder. **Nothing
is sent anywhere**: the game has no network code in it at all. Open it, read
it, and send it or do not, as you like.

If a crash happens the game writes a separate folder and shows you where the
next time you start it. Same rules: plain text, local, yours.
